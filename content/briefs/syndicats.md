**Not one contractor on the page.** Every result explains who is liable. The

somebody has to actually do the work, in an occupied building, and invoice it in
a form the syndicate and its insurer can both process.

**The gap we're filling:** the SERP settles responsibility and stops. This page
starts where it stops — the syndicate has been told it must act, and needs
someone who has done this in a co-ownership before.

## Our angle

CondoLegal's point is the hinge: after a loss it is **the syndicate that must
intervene first** and carry out the work to repair the damage and preserve the
building, and it is the syndicate's insurer that takes charge first. So the
board's next task is procurement, under time pressure, with an adjuster
watching and owners in the corridor asking questions.

That is a narrow, specific brief, and we already meet it in ways the site
states elsewhere but never gathered in one place for this reader: written scope
separating common portions from private ones, photo documentation before and
after, invoicing in the form a syndicate or an insurer needs, containment and
phasing in occupied buildings, liability insurance with certificates on request,
and a one-year written workmanship warranty.

Nothing on this page is a new claim. It is the existing claims, assembled for a
reader who has never been on our site before and has a meeting on Thursday.
