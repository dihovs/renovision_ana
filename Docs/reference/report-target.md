# The report we are copying

**Source:** `My New Project Report 5.pdf`, exported from magicplan by the
owner 20 Aug 2026 and sent as the reference. Read with PDFKit, not eyeballed.
Held for ORD-45 item 1. **Nothing built from this yet** — his instruction was
to wait for the screenshots that go with it.

His own standing rule, 19 Aug: *"just duplicate whatever I send you. Don't
try to be creative whatever."*

## The shape of it

**5 pages, every one 612 × 792 pt — US Letter, portrait.** Ours, from the
same kind of job: **1 page, 681 × 14091 pt.** That is the whole first
problem in one line.

**The company block is the headline difference.** Their cover ends with HIS
firm — name, email, postal address, website, phone. Ours prints `INSURED —`,
`PROPERTY —`, `WORK STARTED —`, `CLAIM DETAILS Not recorded`: four empty
placeholders where their cover puts the one thing that is always known.

`CREATED ON` is the date, not `REPORT PREPARED`.

## Page 2 — the floor

Section rule: `▼ Ground Floor TOTAL AREA: … • LIVING AREA: … • ROOMS: 1`.

The plan, with a full dimension chain — `4.654`, `1.434 3.168`, `2.397
3.385`, `5.501`, `5.530` — and the room labelled in place:

```
Private Office
26.63 m² (4.654 × 5.904)
```

Then a scale bar `0.0 0.5 1.0 1.5m` and a ratio `1:37`.

**Figures on the plan carry no unit** — bare `4.654`. The unit is stated once,
on the scale bar. Ours prints `18'-1 1/2"` on every dimension.

## Page 3 — the room

```
▼ Private Office
Ground Floor
WIDTH: 5.904 m • LENGTH: 4.654 m • CEILING HEIGHT: 3.736 m
AREA: 26.63 m² • PERIMETER: 24.71 m
```

Five figures, in two rows, as running text. **No wall area.** Ours prints a
four-cell strip — Floor · Wall area (gross) · Perimeter · Ceiling height —
and leads with a number theirs does not show at all.

The plan is repeated larger (`1:70`) with the full chain including the
openings' own split segments: `1.688`, `0.788`, `2.966`, `1.798`, `2.126`.

Then, on the SAME page, the damage:

```
▼ Private Office/Ground Floor
1 AFFECTED AREA
Area   14.33 m²
Name   Affected Area 1
Photo  1 Photo (see photos page)
Notes  This floor is completely damaged. The tile needs to come off and we
       have to change the entire floor. We also might need to check what's
       going on under there, with the subfloor
```

Label-and-value rows, the note in full, and the photo **cross-referenced**
rather than inlined — *"1 Photo (see photos page)"*.

Photos grouped under the room, captioned by what they are of.

## What to take from it

1. Real Letter pages. This is the whole of item 1.
2. The cover ends with the firm's own contact block.
3. Room stats are WIDTH · LENGTH · CEILING HEIGHT · AREA · PERIMETER, as
   text. No wall area.
4. Plan dimensions are bare numbers; the unit lives on the scale bar.
5. Affected areas are a labelled block on the room's own page, notes in
   full, photos cross-referenced to a later page.
