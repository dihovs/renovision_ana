**Researched 2026-07-31.** Companion to `Renovision-Ana-SEO-Roadmap.md` and
`Website-Competitive-Research.md`. Sources: ~1,600 Google Autocomplete
suggestions (hl=fr/en, gl=ca), ~35 live google.ca SERPs, Google Trends CA-QC

## Read before writing any page

1. **French autocomplete is ~40% France.** Anything mentioning Veolia, SAUR,
   MAAF, loi Warsmann, garantie décennale, permis de construire, TVA, or
   prix au m² is France-only. Quebec signal = Quebec city, insurer
   (Desjardins/Beneva/Intact/Promutuel/La Personnelle), or legal term (TAL,
   vice caché, loi 16/141). **Always price in $/pi², never m²** — the
   France-scraped content ranking today answers in m², which is our opening.
2. **Bare "dégât d'eau" is not commercial** (dream-interpretation + flood-news
   dilution; Montréal SERP is municipal/news). Commercial value is in modified
   forms: + ville, + quoi faire, + qui appeler, + plafond, + condo, + assurance.
3. **renovisionana.ca appeared in ZERO of ~35 money SERPs** (brand queries:
   #1). Indexed, invisible. Starting line, not a verdict.

## SERP landscape — what we verified live

- **"rénovation après sinistre Laval" is the most winnable P0 in the market.**
  Top 3 = thin locals (terbois, onside, lplconstruction); Soresto #5, Groupe
  RPL #8 — both beatable now. Map pack slot held by a 21-review competitor.
- **"dégât d'eau plafond réparation Montréal": no restoration brand in organic
  top 3** (plasterers + plumbers). A dedicated ceiling page is an open lane.
  Atmospec is in the pack (5.0★/85) but not organic.
- **"urgence dégât d'eau"**: half the SERP is plumbers. Position as the one who
  does what comes *after* the plumber.
- **EN water-damage terms**: national-franchise wall (Steamatic, Paul Davis,
  On Side) + 100–200-review map packs. Long game; FR first.
- **JG Lessard is the local to beat** — #1/#2 on every AUDIENCE term
  (insurance, commercial, gestionnaire, syndicat) because it has audience
  pages. Direct confirmation of competitive-research recs #9/#18.
- **Renovation SERPs are 2–5/8 directories** (soumissionrenovation, 411
  habitation, renoquotes, HomeStars, Houzz, PagesJaunes…). Ceiling is organic
  #3–5 — and claiming the directory profiles is itself a ranking play (free).
- **Stay out of:** infiltration d'eau sous-sol Laval (foundation-crack/French-
  drain vertical — write a handoff section, earn referrals instead);
  refoulement clearing (plumbers); "expert en sinistre" (job-seeker intent);
  bare "dégât d'eau Montréal" (flood news).
