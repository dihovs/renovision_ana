## 1. PERF VERDICT

**Scores (mobile / the ones that matter):** home 89, estimation 96, water-damage 90, blog 84. Desktop home is a perfect 100. SEO 100 and Best Practices 100 on all five runs. CLS ~0 and TBT ≤100 ms everywhere — the entire mobile gap is **LCP render delay** (~2.0–2.3 s of every mobile LCP is paint-gating, not network) plus Speed Index on home.

**Top 3 fixes, ranked by impact × doable-tonight:**

1. **Kill the entrance animation / hydration gate on above-the-fold content.** The LCP element paints ~2 s after its resource arrives on every mobile page — including water-damage, where the LCP is the plain `h1.font-heading` with **no image involved** (2.26 s of pure render delay). That fingerprint = a fade/slide-in entrance effect (or hydration-gated visibility) on hero sections. Find the reveal wrapper (likely a shared hero/section animation component used by home hero, service hero `h1`, and blog header) and make the first-viewport content render visible immediately — animate only below-the-fold, or start at `opacity:1` and animate via CSS that doesn't wait on JS. **Expected gain: up to ~2 s of mobile LCP on every page — the single largest lever on the site**, worth roughly the whole 89→high-90s gap on home and 84→~95 on blog.
2. **`priority` on the LCP images.** Home hero "after" image (`data-nimg=fill`, the finished-basement shot) and the blog header image both fail the `fetchpriority=high` check. Add `priority` to those two `next/image` instances — home hero component (the before/after hero, `src/components/**/HeroBanner*`/hero section) and the blog post header image in the blog post page/component. **Gain: ~200–300 ms off LCP request start**, on top of fix 1.
3. **Blog header image delivery.** `condo-contingency-fund-header.png` is served at w=1920 for a 380×256 slot with `sizes="768px"` — 103 KiB of 162 KiB wasted, image-delivery score 0. Fix the `sizes` attribute to match the real slot, and convert the source PNG in `public/images/blog/` to JPEG/WebP (it's a photo, not line art). While in there: recompress `hero-basement-before-v2.jpg` (31 KiB wasted) and route `vision-ai-mark.png` on /estimation through `next/image` (17 KiB raw for a 20×20 icon). **Gain: ~130 KiB and most of the blog page's 84→90+ recovery.**

Deprioritize: the shared-chunk unused/legacy JS (~26 KiB + 14 KiB polyfills) and the two render-blocking CSS files (~100–150 ms) are real but are build-config surgery, not tonight's ROI. Accessibility is the only category under 100 (92–96): add `inert` to the collapsed mobile-nav drawer and the hidden chat container (both `aria-hidden` with focusable children), and revisit `text-charcoal/40–/60` contrast — cheap, bundle it in.
