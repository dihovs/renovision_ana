**1 · Ceiling water damage.** The single cleanest open lane found in the whole
SERP sweep: no restoration brand holds an organic top-3 spot for "dégât d'eau
plafond réparation Montréal" — it's plasterers and plumbers. Atmospec is in the
map pack but not organic. The PAA hands us the outline verbatim: *que faire si
le plafond est endommagé · comment le réparer · comment le faire sécher · qui
doit payer quand ça vient du condo au-dessus*. That last question is the best
single query in the dataset — emergency, condo, insurance and our own trades in
one sentence — and it links straight to items 2 and 4. Plafond is currently
mentioned only in passing on the drywall, painting and water-damage pages;
nothing owns it. Links: from `/services/water-damage`, to `/services/drywall`
and `/services/painting`.

**2 · Condo — who pays?** De-orphans the Bill 16 post, which currently has no
inbound link from anywhere that converts. Answers the PAA cluster *qui est
responsable · locataire ou propriétaire · parties communes qui paye*. Cite the
Quebec framework (loi 16/141, TAL) — never the French equivalents.

**3 · Insurance claim guide, contractor's side.** Nobody local writes the
claim process from the contractor's chair: what the adjuster needs, what
documentation actually speeds a file, where homeowners lose money. This is the
written form of what the water-damage page already claims about moisture
readings and written scope. Non-seasonal.

**4 · `/syndicats` and 5 · `/gestionnaires`.** JG Lessard ranks #1/#2 on every
audience term in this market for exactly one reason: it has audience pages and
we don't. `/gestionnaires` is the wider-open of the two — that SERP returns one
competitor and then job listings. The calendar puts B2B pages in Sep–Oct
because they're non-seasonal and this is the window that isn't fighting a
weather peak.

**6 · Terrebonne + Rive-Nord.** The nine existing area pages cover sectors that
mostly *don't* autocomplete for water damage; the Rive-Nord cities (Terrebonne,
Blainville, Boisbriand, Saint-Eustache, Repentigny) do. Terrebonne is already
claimed in the LocalBusiness schema with no page behind it. Build Terrebonne
first as a full page, then a Rive-Nord hub. Same discipline as the existing
nine: real sourced local facts, no template swap.

**7 · Mould — blocked.** Seasonal window is Jul–Oct (Trends 47–54) and it is
closing. Blocked on one owner answer: **does Renovision do mould remediation
itself, or refer it out?** If referred, we write a handoff section on the
water-damage page and earn referrals instead — we do not build a page for work
we don't perform.

**8 · `/assureurs` — blocked** on three owner answers: do we use Xactimate, are
we on any insurer panels, do we bill insurers directly. The page is worthless
without them, because those three facts *are* the page.

## Not writing, and why

  vertical. Write a handoff section, earn the referral, don't chase the term.
- **Refoulement clearing** — that's a plumber's job, and the SERP knows it.
- **"Expert en sinistre"** — job-seeker intent, not customer intent.
- **Bare "dégât d'eau Montréal"** — diluted by flood news and municipal pages.
- **Île-Perrot, Longueuil, West Island pages** — claimed in schema, no real
  service depth behind them. Either earn them or trim the schema claim;
  don't paper over it with a thin page.

## Non-content items that gate content

These aren't writing tasks, but they cap what writing can achieve. Owner-only.

- **Directory profiles** (soumissionrenovation, 411 habitation, renoquotes,
  TrustedPros, Houzz, HomeStars, PagesJaunes) — renovation SERPs are 2–5 of 8
  directories. Claiming the profiles is itself a ranking play, and it's free.
- **GBP hours** still say Mon–Fri 8–18 while the site and schema say answered
  24/7. A contradiction Google can see.
- **Facebook page rename** — still `facebook.com/nettoyageana`, still
  outranking the site for the brand name.
- **Review engine** — every emergency query resolves to the map pack, and the
  Laval bar is low (3.9★/18 to 4.9★/30).
