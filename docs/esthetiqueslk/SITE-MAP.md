## Every offer/treatment evidenced by the accessible feed

### Face and skin

- **Extraction / nettoyage en profondeur** — manual extraction for full face and neck; explicitly says it is not superficial. Reels describe about 1h30 and normal redness/bruising/swelling during roughly 3–5 days.
- **Microdermabrasion** — paired with extraction to remove dead cells and improve radiance/skin texture.
- **Peeling chimique** — promoted for acne, acne scars, anti-âge, hyperpigmentation, rosacea, stains and other imperfections; posts describe peeling/healing over about seven days and a smoother, more uniform complexion.
- **Facial / soin du visage** — feed uses `facial`, `nettoyage`, and “Ma passion”; the exact “classic facial” wording is not used in the accessible captions.
- **Anti-âge / lifting / contour** — stated benefits of deep extraction and peeling content, including lifting/contouring and diminishing a double chin.

### Body

- **Remodelage corporel / body contouring** using a Beauty Pot 4-in-1 machine:
  - **lipo cavitation**
  - **radio fréquence**
  - **lipo laser**
  - **lumière LED**
- Claimed goals/results: loss of several inches, destruction of fat cells, skin firming/lifting, instant lymphatic drainage, cellulite treatment, stretch-mark treatment, muscle relaxation, and reduced water retention. These are the account’s promotional claims, not independently verified outcomes.
- **Cellulite treatment** is repeatedly named in captions and highlight labels/results.
- **Lymphatic drainage** is repeatedly named; the current site additionally says pressotherapy, but `pressothérapie` was not found in the accessible Instagram feed.
- **Free consultation** is explicitly offered in the remodelage captions.

### Hair removal / lashes / products (highlight evidence)

- **Lash Lift** is an explicit highlight label. This supports a lash-lift offer, but not necessarily the site’s “extensions de cils” variants.
- **Aisselles** is an explicit highlight label and strongly suggests an underarm hair-removal offer; the highlight content itself is gated for guests, so confirm whether it is waxing, another hair-removal method, or information only.
- **Produits** is an explicit highlight label, indicating products are promoted/sold or discussed; exact brands/products were not visible to a guest.
- **Prix** is an explicit highlight label. One accessible remodelage graphic shows `$140` per session, `$500` for 5, `$900` for 10, taxes included; verify current pricing before publishing.
- **Avis Clientes / Résultats Corps / PEAU / CORPS / INFO** are proof, category, and information highlights rather than additional treatments.

> Instagram highlight pages themselves show a “Sign up to see more story highlights” gate to guests. Therefore the labels above are complete for visible highlight names, but the underlying story cards/products/prices cannot be treated as fully audited without account access.
